01 / The course

Position over power.

Faldo drew a course that thinks. Flat, honest fairways bend around mature trees, open marsh, and the lakes that reach into twelve of the eighteen holes. The greens are small, firm and quick, so the right half of the fairway beats the long half.

Opened in 1998 as Chuan Chuen Golf Club, the course keeps its original bones. The clubhouse was rebuilt in 2018, a new team has run the place since 2023, and the greens now roll as true as anywhere north of the city.

Resident wildlife: egrets, monitor lizards, and the occasional gallery of estate dogs. All are considered spectators.

The cart path crosses water on the way out. So will your round.

    03 / Local rules

    Water, in play.

    Lakes lie along holes 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 18. Play them as lateral hazards.

    On 2, 7, 12, 15 and 18 the water crosses your line. Drop zones are provided; pride is optional.

    And the club's gentlest rule: cultivated flowerbeds are ground under repair. Land among the blossoms and you drop free.

    04 / The name

    A ville of flora, honestly named.

    The estate keeps its word. Tabebuias lean over the tee boxes, lotus opens on the lakes, and the flowerbeds are tended like greens. The course reads less like a property and more like a garden that keeps a scorecard.

    06 / The clubhouse

    Rebuilt in 2018, run like 2026.

    The old clubhouse came down and a pavilion of glass and timber went up, with long views over the eighteenth and cold towels at the door. Since 2023 the polish shows everywhere, from the locker rooms to the practice green.

    Questions, answered

    How far is Flora Ville from Bangkok?

    About 45 minutes from central Bangkok by expressway, and 13 km from Don Mueang airport. The address is 110 Moo 8, Bangkok to Pathum Thani Road, Bang Khu Wat.

    Can I play golf at night?

    Yes. The course is floodlit. Tee off from 15:00 Tuesday to Friday, and from 16:00 on Wednesdays, Saturdays and holidays. The last groups leave at 18:00.

    How much does a round cost?

    Agent packages with 18 holes, a caddie and a cart start around ฿2,900 on weekdays and ฿3,100 on weekends. Caddie fee ฿400, single cart ฿700. Confirm rates when you book.

    How do I book a tee time?

    Call +66 2-598-2555, message LINE @floravillegolf, or email sale.1@floravillegolf.com. Walk-ins should call one day ahead.

    What is the dress code?

    Collared shirts and tailored shorts or slacks, with soft spike golf shoes. No denim on the course. Smart casual in the clubhouse.
